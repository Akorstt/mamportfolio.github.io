01 About me

A curious
work in
progress.

I'm studying architecture at ENA Rabat, and I'm interested in the space where the built world and digital tools meet.

I'm exploring how code, computational design, and better workflows can change the way we think about and make things. I haven't picked one narrow direction, and that's part of the point: I'm learning by trying.

Outside of that, I enjoy games, creating levels, football, and finding new things to make. This site is where the interesting bits can live as they develop.
